# PayAviator — Store Listing Working Draft

This is an internal draft for a future store listing. It has not been submitted. It is not a privacy policy and it is not legal advice. Wording below is limited to behavior checked in this repository. See [PRIVACY_TERMS_AUDIT_2026-11-07.md](PRIVACY_TERMS_AUDIT_2026-11-07.md) before copying any sentence to Play Console.

## Name
PayAviator

## Short description
Track bills, income, spending and reminders on your phone. Bank linking and cloud sync are optional.

## Full description
PayAviator is a personal budgeting and bill-tracking app. You can track recurring and one-time bills, income, spending, refunds and a calendar. Reminders are optional and are scheduled on the phone. The phone can delay or skip them. They do not pay a bill.

Bills, income and spending are stored on the device. You can use those features without an account. If you sign in with a verified email, the app can also keep a copy on PayAviator's servers so it can sync. Connecting a bank is optional, needs an account, and uses read-only access through Plaid. The app does not ask you to type your bank password into PayAviator.

The current source shows PayAviator Plus at $3.99 a month or $29.99 a year. Purchases are not turned on in this source (`BILLING_LIVE` is false). Do not write that the Android app sells subscriptions until Play Billing is actually wired and a build with that behavior is the one being listed.

Safe to Spend and payoff figures are estimates from the information in the app. They can be wrong. PayAviator is not a bank, lender, payment processor, investment service, or financial adviser.

You can export a backup when you choose to. Uninstalling the app does not delete backup files you saved somewhere else.

## Do not claim in the listing
- That the app works with no Internet permission, or that it has no accounts. Internet is used for optional sign-in, bank linking, cloud sync and bank-reconnect notifications.
- That reminders are exact or that a due date cannot be missed.
- That security, balances or calculations are guaranteed.
- An age gate, a registered company name, a legal address, or a compliance badge, unless those are separately verified.
- That "Ask PayAviator" or any other assistant is part of the app. It is not in this source.
